What a packet actually does
Minecraft → 127.0.0.1:25565 (friend's app) → box · 100.64.0.1:25565 (wireguard) → host's app → 192.168.0.10:25565

The friend's game never leaves their machine. It talks to a real local socket. Everything past that is the app: encrypted to the box address, accepted only on a port you published, then dialled on your LAN. Your real IP, router and other devices are never on the path.
